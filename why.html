The Potential Equation

The friction is the point.

Character defects aren’t moral failures. Fear, pride, resentment — these are just the ego doing what it was built to do: protect you. The problem is, those same defenses became the thing that almost killed you.

Choosing a spiritual principle over a character defect is hard. It creates friction. It’s supposed to. Every time you choose humility over pride, or gratitude over self-pity, that’s a small act of courage that most people around you will never fully see.

The AI doesn’t judge the friction. It helps you map it — so you can see your progress even on the days it feels like you’re standing still.

That’s what the Recovery Transparency Index does. It’s not a score that grades your worth. It’s a compass that shows you which direction you’ve been walking.
