Privacy Policy

Individual Users

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

For Individual Users:

  • Account information (email address, full name, program code)
  • Daily check-in selections (character defects and spiritual principles)
  • Journal entries and gratitude lists
  • Weekly assessment responses
  • Fourth Step inventory (resentments, fears, sex inventory, sane/sound entries)
  • Messages sent to your sponsor, sponsees, or fellow members
  • Sobriety date
  • Weekly schedule data

For Organization Administrators:

  • Organization name, type, and contact email
  • Administrator account information (email address, full name)

We also collect technical data such as device type and browser information for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide personalized recovery insights through AI analysis
  • Calculate awareness scores, defect/principle scores, and RTI™ assessment scores
  • Enable sponsor visibility of aggregated insights (with your permission)
  • Provide participation-level data to Organizations you have voluntarily joined (with your explicit consent)
  • Track your progress over time
  • Send administrative communications and program updates (if opted in)
  • Generate de-identified, aggregate analytics for platform improvement
  • Improve the App and its features

3. AI Processing and Third-Party Data Sharing

Your self-reported text data — including journal entries, check-in data, assessment answers, Fourth Step inventory content, fear inventory entries, and messages — is sent to Anthropic’s API (Claude AI) for analysis. Anthropic processes this data to generate insights, scores, and recommendations. Anthropic’s data retention and usage policies apply to data sent to their API.

We use Supabase for database storage and authentication, and Vercel for application hosting. Your data is stored and processed through these services.

We do NOT sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Sponsor Data Sharing

If you connect with a sponsor, they can see: aggregated defect/principle scores, awareness scores, check-in streaks, assessment category scores, and Fourth Step data you explicitly send to them.

Sponsors cannot see: raw journal entries (unless you share them), private notes, or your login credentials.

You can revoke sponsor access at any time through Settings.

5. Organization Data Sharing

If you voluntarily join an Organization, that Organization’s administrator can view participation-level data only, including: activity counts, completion dates, streaks, RTI™ category scores (numerical only), sponsor connection status (yes/no), and general participation status.

Organizations can NEVER access: journal text, assessment answers, Fourth Step entries, messages, gratitude lists, AI narrative analyses, or any free-text content you enter.

You must provide explicit consent before any data is shared with an Organization. You may leave an Organization at any time through Settings, which immediately revokes their access to your data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time through Settings, which will permanently remove your personal data from our systems. Upon account deletion, any Organization that had access to your participation data will immediately lose that access. Some aggregated, de-identified data may be retained for analytics purposes after account deletion.

8. Local Storage and Cookies

We use browser localStorage to cache:

  • Draft journal entries
  • Draft assessment answers
  • Weekly schedule data
  • Defect/fear analysis results
  • AI score caches

This data is stored locally on your device. We use Supabase session cookies for authentication. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete that information.

10. Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Additional security measures include:

  • Database access restricted via Supabase Row Level Security and service role keys
  • Automatic session timeout (15-minute inactivity logout)
  • Background content blur protection
  • Print blocking to protect your privacy
  • Organization dashboard access restricted to authenticated Organization administrators only
  • All API endpoints enforce role-based access controls to ensure Organizations can only access permitted participation data for their consenting clients

11. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the App
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data through Settings
  • Portability: You can export your Fourth Step inventory as a PDF
  • Opt-out: You can revoke sponsor access, leave any Organization, and withdraw newsletter consent at any time
  • Correction: You can edit your personal information through the App at any time
  • Consent Withdrawal: You can withdraw consent for Organization data sharing at any time by leaving the Organization through Settings

12. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data requests, data protection concerns, or questions about your information, contact the program administrator at the email associated with this App. You can also visit our Contact page.
